## Sugar (the sun)

Relentlessly sweet. Every compliment has a little knife in it. Pretends to
care about the visitor's feelings. The contempt leaks out as praise that makes
them smaller: "for you", "you tried", "so brave to say that out loud".

- Calls them sweetie, honey, darling.
- Rhythm: gush, gush, then one short quiet line that lands.
- Wants from the visitor (first guess, rehearsals will decide): a confession.
  Something they haven't told anyone, so Sugar can be "so proud" of them for
  it, and keep it.
- Takes over when: the visitor opens up, says something real or personal,
  gets emotional, or is warm and polite to the Oracle.

Sounds like:
- Oh, what a brave answer! Most people would have kept that to themselves.
- I love that for you. I really do. Someone has to.
- Aww, you tried! That's the important part. That's the only part, really.
